## Overview
`Ag` is governed by the dative case.

| Pronoun      | Form   | Contrast Form | Translation |
|:-------------|:-------|:--------------|:------------|
| **I**        | agam   | agamsa        | at me       |
| **you**      | agat   | agatsa        | at you      |
| **he**       | aige   | aigesean      | at him      |
| **she**      | aici   | aicise        | at her      |
| **we**       | againn | againne       | at us       |
| **you (pl)** | agaibh | agaibhse      | at you      |
| **they**     | acu    | acusan        | at them     |

## Interrogatives

| Structure    | Following Clause  | Translation        | Example                   | Translation              |
|:-------------|:------------------|:-------------------|:--------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Cé + ag**  | Indirect relative | by whom?           | Cé aige a bhfuil sé?      | Who has it?              |
| **Cad + ag** | Indirect relative | at what?           | Cad aige a bhfuil sé?     | Who has it?              |
| **Cé + ag**  | Direct relative   | which?             | Cé acu is fearr leat?     | Which do you prefer?     |
| **Cé + ag**  | Direct relative   | who of?            | Cé againn a bheidh ansin? | Who of us will be there? |

## Advanced uses

<LogicSection title="Possession & Knowledge">
    - **Possession:** *Tá leabhar agam* (I have a book).
    - **Knowledge:** *Tá Gaeilge aige* (He knows Irish).
    - **Ability:** *Tá snámh aici* (She can swim).
</LogicSection>

<LogicSection title="Spatial & Temporal">
    - **Place:** *Ag an doras* (At the door).
    - **Time:** *Ag am dinnéir* (At dinner time).
    - **Events:** *Ag an rince* (At the dance).
</LogicSection>

<LogicSection title="Advanced Grammatical Functions">
    - **Verbal Noun:** *Ag obair* (Working).
    - **The Partitive:** *Duine acu* (One of them).
    - **Genitive Substitute:** *Mac ag an rí* (A son of the king).
</LogicSection>
