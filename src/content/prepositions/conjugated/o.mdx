## Overview

### Forms

| Pronoun      | Form   | Contrast Form | Translation |
|:-------------|:-------|:--------------|:------------|
| **I**        | uaim   | uaimse        | from me     |
| **You**      | uait   | uaitse        | from you    |
| **He**       | uaidh  | uaidhsean     | from him    |
| **She**      | uaithi | uaithise      | from her    |
| **We**       | uainn  | uainne        | from us     |
| **You (pl)** | uaibh  | uaibhse       | from you    |
| **They**     | uathu  | uathusan      | from them   |

### Possessive Combinations

| Pronoun       | Combination | Translation |

### Mutations

<MutationCard before={"Ó + an + Gearmáin"} after={"Ón nGearmáin"} title={"With an article"} rule={"Eclipsis"}/>

* **General Rule** — *ó Chiarraí* — from Kerry — Without the article, *ó* causes **lenition**.
* **With the Article** — *ón nGearmáin* — from Germany — *Ó* combines with *an* to form **ón**, generally causing **eclipsis**.
  * **Munster Dialect**: Eclipses *d* and *t* as well.
  * **Connacht Dialect**: Adds a **t-prefix** to feminine nouns starting with *s* (e.g., *ón tsúil*).
  * **Ulster Dialect**: Uses **lenition** instead of eclipsis (e.g., *ón Ghearmáin*).

### Interrogatives

| Question      | Meaning    | What happens next? | Example                   |
|:--------------|:-----------|:-------------------|:--------------------------|
| **Cé uaidh**  | From whom? | `Indirect`         | Cé uaidh an litir seo?    |
| **Cad uaidh** | From what? | `Indirect`         | Cad uaidh ar tháinig sin? |

## Wants & Needs

Ó is used for what you want or need:
`Teastaíonn + <something> + ó + <someone>`

* *Teastaíonn spóirt uaim* — I want fun/sport (Fun is needed from me).
* *Tá bainne uait* — You need milk.
* *Teastaíonn uaim labhairt leat* — I want to speak with you.

## Origin, Time & Separation

### Spatial & Abstract Origin
* **Spatial Origin** — *tar ón bposta* — come from the post office.
* **Geographic Origin** — *fear ó Thrá Lí* — a man from Tralee.
* **Source** — *litir uait* — a letter from you.
  * *cabhair ó chairde* — help from friends.

### Time & Duration
* **Since (Point in Time)** — *ón Nollaig* — since Christmas.
  * *ó mhaidin* — since this morning.
* **Conjunction (Since/Because)** — *ó bhí sí tinn* — since (because) she was sick.
* **Range** — *ó thús go deireadh* — from beginning to end.
  * *ó lá go lá* — from day to day.

### Separation & Absence
* **Distance** — *míle uaidh* — a mile (away) from it.
* **Absence** — *is fearr bean uaidh* — (he is) better off **without** a wife (lit. a wife from him).
* **Freedom** — *bí ó bhaol* — to be free from danger.

## Causes & Qualities

### Cause
* **Physical Cause** — *bodhar ó thorann* — deaf from the noise.
  * *tuirseach ó shiúl* — tired from walking.

### Judgment & Adjectives
* **Behavior** — *deas uait* — nice of you.
* **Ability** — *is breá uait* — you are good at / capable of.

### Mathematics
* **Subtraction** — *a dó óna trí sin a haon* — 3 minus 2 is 1.
  * *Literal Translation*: "Two from three, that is one."
