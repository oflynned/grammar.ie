## Overview

The preposition `as` indicates movement 'out of' or origin 'from'. Historically, it was often written as `a`, which can still be seen in older texts and some dialects. In some Munster dialects, `a` (often written `a'`) is still used before a consonant, e.g., *a' ceann*.

`As` requires the dative case.

### Conjugated Forms

When combined with personal pronouns, `as` takes the following forms:

| Person   | Standard Form | Emphatic Form | Translation     |
|:---------|:--------------|:--------------|:----------------|
| I        | `asam`        | `asamsa`      | out of me       |
| You (sg) | `asat`        | `asatsa`      | out of you      |
| He / It  | `as`          | `as-san`      | out of him / it |
| She      | `aisti`       | `aistise`     | out of her      |
| We       | `asainn`      | `asainne`     | out of us       |
| You (pl) | `asaibh`      | `asaibhse`    | out of you      |
| They     | `astu`        | `astusan`     | out of them     |

### Question Forms

*   `cé as`? = from whom?
*   `cad as` / `cé as` / `cá has`? = from what?
*   `cad as` / `cé as` / `cá has`? = where is ... from? (in a copular clause)

## Core Functions: Movement and Origin

### Spatial Movement
Describes movement 'out of' a place, state, or situation.
*   *as guais* = out of danger
*   *as an imirt* = out of the game

### Origin
Indicates where a person or thing is from, typically used with the copula.
*   *Is as Béal Feirste é.* = He is from Belfast.

### Starting Point
Marks the beginning of a journey or range.
*   *as sin go hÉirinn* = from there to Ireland
*   *i bhfad as seo* = far from here

### Selection
Used to denote choosing one thing from a group.
*   *bain rogha as* = to select from
*   *duine as béirt* = one of two

## Source and Composition

### Material Composition
Indicates the material something is made 'of'.
*   *as an éadach* = of the fabric

### Source of Information or Action
Identifies the source of a perception, name, conclusion, or emotion.
*   **Perception**: *Tá baladh as an madadh.* = The dog smells.
*   **Name**: *Baisteadh as a athair é.* = He was named after his father.
*   **Knowledge**: *Aithním as a shúil é.* = I recognised him by his walk.
*   **Conclusion**: *Tuigim as.* = From that I understand.
*   **Emotion**: *as fearg* = out of anger.

### Idiomatic Sources
`As` is a key part of several common idiomatic structures.

| Concept | Formula                     | Example                    | Translation (Literal)                    |
|:--------|:----------------------------|:---------------------------|:-----------------------------------------|
| Pride   | `Tá bród orm asat`          | *I am proud of you.*       | Is pride on-me out-of-you                |
| Thanks  | `Go raibh maith agat as...` | *Thank you for this work.* | That be good at-you out-of the work this |
| Trust   | `Tá muinín agam aisti`      | *I trust her.*             | Is trust at-me out-of-her                |

## State and Condition

### Being Outside a State
`As` can describe being outside of a certain condition or range.
*   **General**: *as amharc* = out of sight
*   **Functional**: *as ordú* = out of order
*   **Employment**: *as obair* = without work / unemployed
*   **Condition**: *as cuimse* = beyond measure

### Being Off or Out
Used adverbially to mean 'off' or 'out'.
*   **To be off (deactivated)**: *Tá an raidió as.* = The radio is off.
*   **To turn off**: *Chas mé an tine as.* = I turned the fire off.
*   **To be out (not present)**: *Tá mé as.* = I am out.

### Being Alone
Used with `féin` to express being alone.
*   *Tá mé ag siúl as féin.* = I am walking alone.
*   *Tá asainn féin anois.* = We are alone now.

## Other Uses

### Medium or Language
Indicates the medium used for an action or the language something is in.
*   *nigh as uisce é* = wash it in (with) water
*   *as Gaeilge* = in Irish

### Specific Location
Specifies a quality at a particular point on an object.
*   *géar as an mbarr* = sharp at the tip

### Involvement
Indicates being involved in something.
*   *tá as rud* = to be involved in something
