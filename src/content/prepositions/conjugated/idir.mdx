## Overview

The preposition `idir` primarily means "between" or "among". Its conjugated forms only exist for the plural pronouns.

| Person    | Standard | Contrastive | Translation  |
|:----------|:---------|:------------|:-------------|
| We        | eadrainn | eadrainne   | between us   |
| You (pl.) | eadraibh | eadraibhse  | between you  |
| They      | eatarthu | eatarthusan | between them |

Singular forms like `eadram` (between me) or `eadrat` (between you) are now obsolete. Instead, the preposition is used with standalone pronouns:
- *idir mise is tusa* - between me and you

In Munster and Connacht, the plural conjugated forms are not used in enumerations with `agus`:
- *idir iad agus na daoine eile* - between them and the other people

### Dialectal Variants
- **Ulster:** `eadar`
- **Connacht:** `eidir`
- **Munster:** `ideir`

### Question Forms
- **Between whom...?**: `cé eatarthu` (+ indirect relative clause)?
- **Between what...?**: `cad eatarthu` / `cé eatarthu` / `cá (h)eatarthu` (+ indirect relative clause)?

## Case and Mutations

### Case
`Idir` requires the following case:
- **Singular:** Accusative
- **Plural:** Dative

### Mutations Without the Article

The mutation following `idir` depends on its meaning.

#### Meaning "between"
When `idir` indicates distance, a period of time, difference, or contrast, it causes no initial mutation.
- *idir Corcaigh agus Baile Átha Cliath* - between Cork and Dublin

#### Meaning "among" or "both...and"
When `idir` means "among", "both...and", or "partly...partly", it causes lenition. This lenition also applies to the word following `agus` or `is`.
- *idir mhná* - among women
- *idir fhear is bhean* - both man and woman

An exception is that lenition does not occur after `agus` or `is` on words beginning with d, t, or s.
- *idir shúgradh agus dáiríre* - half in jest, half in earnest

In Cork, lenition typically occurs only after `idir` and not after the following `agus`.

### Mutations With the Article
When `idir` is followed by the definite article `an` or `na`, standard mutation rules for the article apply.

- **Singular:** The rules are the same as for the nominative/accusative singular.
  - *idir an t-oileán agus an mhór-roinn* - between the island and the mainland
- **Plural:** The rules are the same as for the nominative/accusative/dative plural, causing h-prothesis before a vowel.
  - *idir na hÉireannaigh* - between the Irish

## Core Functions

### Spatial and Temporal
`Idir` is used to describe a position or time between two points.
- *idir Baile Átha Cliath agus Gaillimh* - between Dublin and Galway
- *idir an dó agus an trí* - between two and three o'clock

### Figurative and Enumerative
The preposition can express a relationship between concepts or create a paired list.
- **Figurative:** *cogadh idir Sasana agus an Fhrainc* - war between England and France
- **Both...and:** *idir shean agus óg* - both young and old
- **Partly...partly:** *idir mhagadh is dáiríre* - half in jest, half in earnest

## Collective and Idiomatic Usage

### Among and In the Midst Of
`Idir` can refer to something shared within or being part of a group.
- **Exclusively among:** *rún idir mhná* - a secret among women
- **In the midst of:** *an bhean is áille idir Éireannaigh* - the most beautiful woman among the Irish

### Idiomatic Expressions
`Idir` is a component of several common idioms.
- *idir an dá linn* - in the meantime
- *idir amanna* - now and then
- *idir eatarthu* - half and half
- *idir lámha* - in progress
- *idir chamáin* - under discussion
