## Overview

### Forms

| Pronoun      | Form     | Contrast Form | Translation |
|:-------------|:---------|:--------------|:------------|
| **I**        | romham   | romhamsa      | before me   |
| **You**      | romhat   | romhatsa      | before you  |
| **He**       | roimhe   | roimhesean    | before him  |
| **She**      | roimpi   | roimpise      | before her  |
| **We**       | romhainn | romhainne     | before us   |
| **You (pl)** | romhaibh | romhaibhse    | before you  |
| **They**     | rompu    | rompusan      | before them |

### Mutations

<MutationCard before={"Roimh + maidin"} after={"Roimh mhaidin"} title={"Without an article"} rule={"Lenition"}/>

<MutationCard before={"Roimh + an + bus"} after={"Roimh an mbus"} title={"With an article"} rule={"Eclipsis"}/>

* **General Rule** — *roimh mhaidin* — before morning — Without the article, *roimh* causes **lenition**.
* **With the Article (General)** — *roimh an mbus* — before the bus — With the article, *roimh* generally causes **eclipsis** (except on d, t).
  * **Munster Dialect**: Eclipses even *d* and *t* (e.g., *roimis an ndalta*).
  * **Connacht Dialect**: Adds a **t-prefix** to feminine nouns starting with *s* (e.g., *roimh an tsúil*).
  * **Ulster Dialect**: Uses **lenition** instead of eclipsis (e.g., *roimh an bhus*).

## Usage

### Time & Sequence
* **Temporal** — *roimh mhaidin* — before morning.
  * *tá mé roimhe ann* — I am there before him (earlier than him).

### Emotions
* **Fear** — *tá faitíos agam roimh an madra* — I am afraid of the dog.
  * *Lit:* "There is fear at-me before the dog."
* **Welcome** — *tá fáilte agam romhat* — I welcome you.

### Action & Prevention
* **Interception** — *tar roimh* / *téigh roimh* — to intercept, stop, prevent, or interrupt.

### Space
* **Spatial** — *roimh* can mean physically "before" or "in front of", but this is rare; *os comhair* is more commonly used for this meaning.
