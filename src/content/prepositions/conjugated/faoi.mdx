## Overview

The preposition `faoi` primarily means "under" or "about". In Connacht and Ulster, it has absorbed many meanings from the older preposition `um`.

Historically, the preposition `um` evolved into `faoi` in many dialects. In Connacht, `faoi` is always used. In Ulster, a distinction is maintained: `faoi` means "under", while `fá` means "about" or "around".

### Forms and Variants

*   **Standard:** `faoi`
*   **Munster:** `fé`
*   **Ulster:** `faoi` (under) and `fá` (about/around)
*   **Older Forms:** `fá`, `fó`, `fí`

### Interrogatives

*   **Under whom...?**: `cé faoi` (+ indirect relative clause)
*   **Under what...?**: `cad faoi` / `cé faoi` / `cá faoi` (+ indirect relative clause)

### Combinations with the Article

*   `faoi` + `an` = `faoin`
*   `faoi` + `na` = `faoi na`

### Dialectal Mutations

*   **Munster:** Eclipsis applies to all consonants, including `d` and `t`.
*   **Connacht:** T-prothesis occurs before feminine nouns beginning with `s-`.
    *   *faoin tsúil = under the eye*
*   **Ulster:** Lenition is always used with the article, instead of eclipsis.
    *   *faoin bhord = under the table*

## Spatial and Temporal Functions

### Spatial Location

`Faoi` is used to denote being physically under or beneath something.

*   *faoin gcathaoir = under the chair*
*   *faoi uisce = under water*
*   *faoi thalamh = under the ground*

It can also mean "on the leeward side of" or indicate a direction.

*   *faoin oileán = on the leeward side of the island*
*   *faoin dtuath = to/in the countryside*

### Temporal Location

`Faoi` can specify a point in time, meaning "around", "at", or "in".

*   *faoi Nollaig = at Christmas*
*   *faoi Bhealtaine = in May*
*   *faoin am seo = around this time*

## State and Condition

`Faoi` can describe a state of being, often translated as "in" or "under".

*   *faoi bhrón = sad (lit. under sorrow)*
*   *faoi shéan = prosperous/happy (lit. under a good sign)*
*   *faoi bhláth = in bloom (lit. under blossom)*
*   *faoi sholas an lae = in the light of day*

It is also used for supporting or bearing a load.

*   *bheith faoin ualach = to bear a load*
*   *na colúin atá faoi = the pillars that support it*

## Topic and Intention

### Topic

`Faoi` is commonly used to mean "about" when discussing a topic.

*   *caint faoi … = to speak about…*
*   *imní faoi … = worry about…*
*   *áthas faoi … = joy about…*
*   *magadh faoi … = mockery of…*

### Motive Force

The preposition can indicate an ongoing action or state of motion.

*   *tá siúl fúm = I am walking quickly*
*   *tá fuadar fút = you are in a hurry*
*   *tá fás faoin gcrann = the tree is growing*

### Intention

The structure `tá fúm...` is a common idiom for expressing intention.

| Concept | Formula | Example | Translation (Literal) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Intention | `Tá` + `faoi` (conjugated) + verbal noun | *Tá fút teacht.* | You intend to come. (Is under you to come.) |

## Numerical Usage

`Faoi` is used to express multiplication or repetition.

*   *faoi dhó = twice*
*   *faoi sé = six times*
*   *a trí faoina ceathair sin a dó dhéag = three times four is twelve*

## Relative Forms

`Faoi` can combine with the relative pronoun `a` and the copula. These forms are less common in everyday speech.

| Tense | Relative Pronoun | Copula (before consonant) | Copula (before vowel) | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Present | `faoina` | `faoinar` | `faoinarb` | under which |
| Past | `faoinar` | `faoinar` | `faoinarbh` | under which |

*   *An chathaoir faoina bhfuil mo leabhar = The chair under which my book is.*
*   *An crann faoina raibh fás = The tree that was growing (lit. the tree under which there was growth).*

The older form `fá` can appear without the linking `n`.

*   *A Róisín, ná bíodh brón ort fár éirigh duit. = My little Rose, do not be sad about what happened to you.*
