## Overview

### Forms

| Pronoun      | Form    | Contrast Form | Translation  |
|:-------------|:--------|:--------------|:-------------|
| **I**        | tríom   | tríomsa       | through me   |
| **You**      | tríot   | tríotsa       | through you  |
| **He**       | tríd    | trísean       | through him  |
| **She**      | tríthi  | tríthise      | through her  |
| **We**       | trínn   | trínne        | through us   |
| **You (pl)** | tríbh   | tríbhse       | through you  |
| **They**     | tríothu | tríothusan    | through them |

**Lenition:** Trí and its forms are often lenited (e.g., `thrí` instead of `trí`).

* **General Rule** — *trí fhuinneoig* — through a window — Without the article, *trí* causes **lenition**.
* **With the Article** — *tríd an bhfuinneoig* — through the window — *Trí* combines with *an* to form **tríd an**, generally causing **eclipsis**.
  * **Munster Dialect**: Eclipses *d* and *t* as well.
  * **Connacht Dialect**: Adds a **t-prefix** to feminine nouns starting with *s* (e.g., *tríd an tsúil*).
  * **Ulster Dialect**: Uses **lenition** instead of eclipsis (e.g., *fríd an fhuinneog*).

### Interrogatives

## Trí vs. Trasna
While both can mean "through" or "across", Irish distinguishes the type of space being traversed.

**Trí** (Penetration): Used for passing through an opening or an enclosed space.
* *Trí fhuinneoig* (Through a window).

**Trasna** (Crossing): Used for crossing open ground or surfaces.
* *Trasna garraí* (Across/through a field).

## Physical World

### Penetration & Space
* **Passing Through** — *trí fhuinneoig* — through a window.
* **Solid Objects** — *chuaigh an piléar trína gualainn* — the bullet went through her shoulder.

### Mixture & Arrangement
* **Among** — *tá bó amháin trí na caorigh* — there is one cow among (mixed through) the sheep.
* **Mixing** — *cuir ola tríd* — mix oil with it (put oil through it).
  * *beoir trí fhíon* — beer mixed with wine.
* **Disorder** — *trína chéile* — confused / mixed up / upset (lit. "through each other").

## Abstract Usage

### Time & Duration
* **Duration** — *tríd an oíche* — through/throughout the night.
* **Continuity** — *tríd an scéal* — throughout the story / while the story was happening.
* **Consistency** — *tríd síos* — throughout / all the way through.

### Cause & Means
* **Cause (Natural)** — *trí bháisteach* — through/because of the rain.
* **Cause (Personal)** — *tríomsa* — because of me.
* **Method (By means of)** — *tríd é a dhéanamh* — by doing it.
* **Medium** — *múineadh trí Ghaeilge* — teaching through Irish (through the medium of).

### States
* **On fire** — *trí thine* — on fire (lit. through fire).
* **Summary** — *trí gach scéal* — in summary / all in all.
