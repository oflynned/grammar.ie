## Overview

The preposition `trí` primarily means "through". It requires the dative case.

A common lenited form, `thrí`, is often used for `trí` and its conjugated forms. Dialectal variants include `tré`, `thré`, and `fríd` (Ulster).

### Conjugated and Possessive Forms

| Person    | Standard Form | Emphatic Form | English      | Possessive Form | English       |
|:----------|:--------------|:--------------|:-------------|:----------------|:--------------|
| I         | `tríom`       | `tríomsa`     | through me   | `trí mo`        | through my    |
| You (sg.) | `tríot`       | `tríotsa`     | through you  | `trí do`        | through your  |
| He        | `tríd`        | `trísean`     | through him  | `trína`         | through his   |
| She       | `tríthi`      | `tríthise`    | through her  | `trína`         | through her   |
| We        | `trínn`       | `trínne`      | through us   | `trínár`        | through our   |
| You (pl.) | `tríbh`       | `tríbhse`     | through you  | `trí bhur`      | through your  |
| They      | `tríothu`     | `tríothusan`  | through them | `trína`         | through their |

### Relative and Copula Forms

| Tense   | Relative Pronoun (`trí` + `a`/`ar`) | Copula Form (`trí` + `ar`) | Copula Form (before a vowel) | English       |
|:--------|:------------------------------------|:---------------------------|:-----------------------------|:--------------|
| Present | `trína`                             | `trínar`                   | `trínarb`                    | through which |
| Past    | `trínar`                            | `trínar`                   | `trínarbh`                   | through which |

### Question Forms

The preposition is used to form questions about the means or medium through which something occurs.

-   `cé tríd` (+ indirect relative clause)? = through whom?
-   `cad tríd` / `cé tríd` / `cá tríd` (+ indirect relative clause)? = through what? / by what means?

## Mutations and Forms with the Article

The form of `trí` and the mutation it causes depends on the presence of the definite article `an`.

-   `trí` + `an` = `tríd an`
-   `trí` + `na` = `trí na`

### Dialectal Notes on Mutations

-   **Munster**: `d` and `t` are also eclipsed after `tríd an`.
-   **Connacht**: T-prothesis occurs before feminine nouns beginning with `s-` (e.g., *tríd an tsúil*).
-   **Ulster**: Lenition is always used instead of eclipsis after the article (e.g., *fríd an fhuinneog*).

## Core Functions

### Penetration of Space

`trí` describes movement through a space, opening, or solid object. For open terrain like a field, `trasna` (across) is more common.

-   *Chuaigh an piléar trína gualainn.* - The bullet went through her shoulder.
-   *Shiúil sé trí fhuinneoig.* - He walked through a window.

### Temporal Duration

It can also express the duration or passage of time, meaning "through" or "during".

-   *D'fhan siad ag caint tríd an oíche.* - They stayed talking through the night.
-   *D'inis sé magadh tríd an scéal.* - He told a joke during the story.

## Agency and State

### Cause and Means

`trí` is used to indicate the cause of something or the means by which an action is performed.

-   *Trí bháisteach a bhí an féar fliuch.* - The grass was wet because of the rain.
-   *Rinneadh é tríomsa.* - It was done because of me.
-   *Foghlaimítear é tríd é a dhéanamh.* - One learns it by doing it.
-   *Múineadh trí Ghaeilge.* - To teach in (by means of) Irish.

### Composition and Mixture

This preposition can describe something being among, between, or mixed with other things.

-   *Tá bó amháin trí na caorigh.* - There is one cow among the sheep.
-   *Cuir beagán ola tríd.* - Mix a little oil with it.
-   *D'ól sé beoir trí fhíon.* - He drank beer with wine.

## Idiomatic Expressions

`trí` appears in several common idioms to express a state or condition.

-   *trí thine* - on fire
-   *trí gach scéal* - in summary
-   *trína chéile* - confused, upset, in disorder
-   *tríd síos* - throughout
