The preposition `faoi` primarily means "under" or "about". It is a simple preposition that governs the dative case, though this is only noticeable in a few specific noun phrases today.

## Initial Mutations

The initial mutation caused by `faoi` depends on whether it is followed by the definite article.

### Dialectal Differences in Mutation

The mutation rules for `faoin` vary across dialects:

*   **Standard/Connacht**: Eclipsis applies to all consonants except `d` and `t`. Feminine nouns starting with `s` get a t-prefix: *faoin tsúil* (under the eye).
*   **Munster**: Eclipsis applies to all consonants, including `d` and `t`.
*   **Ulster**: Lenition is used instead of eclipsis: *faoin bhord* (under the table).

## Forms of 'faoi'

### With the Definite Article

*   `faoi` + `an` (singular) becomes `faoin`.
*   `faoi` + `na` (plural) remains `faoi na`.

### Conjugated Forms (Prepositional Pronouns)

When `faoi` is combined with a personal pronoun, it forms a prepositional pronoun.

| Person | Standard Form | Emphatic Form | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1st sg. | `fúm` | `fúmsa` | under me |
| 2nd sg. | `fút` | `fútsa` | under you |
| 3rd sg. (m) | `faoi` | `faoisean` | under him |
| 3rd sg. (f) | `fúithi` | `fúithise` | under her |
| 1st pl. | `fúinn` | `fúinne` | under us |
| 2nd pl. | `fúibh` | `fúibhse` | under you |
| 3rd pl. | `fúthu` | `fúthusan` | under them |

### With Possessive Pronouns

`faoi` combines directly with possessive pronouns.

| Person | Form | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1st sg. | `faoi mo` | under my |
| 2nd sg. | `faoi do` | under your |
| 3rd sg. (m) | `faoina` | under his |
| 3rd sg. (f) | `faoina` | under her |
| 1st pl. | `faoinár` | under our |
| 2nd pl. | `faoi bhur` | under your |
| 3rd pl. | `faoina` | under their |

## Usage and Meanings

`faoi` has a wide range of uses beyond its literal spatial meaning.

### Spatial Meaning: 'under'

This is the most common meaning of `faoi`.
*   *faoin gcathaoir* - under the chair
*   *faoi uisce* - under water
*   *faoi thalamh* - underground
*   *faoin oileán* - on the leeward side of the island

It can also indicate direction towards a place.
*   *faoin dtuath* - to/in the countryside

### Topic: 'about'

`faoi` is used to introduce the topic of a conversation, thought, or feeling.
*   *caint faoi...* - to speak about...
*   *imní faoi...* - worry about...
*   *áthas faoi...* - joy about...
*   *brón faoi...* - sorrow for/about...
*   *magadh faoi...* - mockery of...
*   *gáire faoi...* - laughter about...

### Expressing Intention

The construction `Tá fúm...` means "I intend to...".
*   *Tá fúm imeacht.* - I intend to leave.
*   *Tá fút teacht.* - You intend to come.

### Expressing Motion or Urgency

`faoi` can describe an underlying force causing movement, growth, or haste.
*   *Tá siúl fúm.* - I am walking quickly.
*   *Tá fuadar fút.* - You are in a hurry.
*   *Tá fás faoin gcrann.* - The tree is growing.

### Describing a State or Condition

It can mean "covered by" or "in a state of".
*   *faoi bhláth* - in bloom (literally "under bloom")
*   *faoi sholas an lae* - in daylight
*   *faoi bhrón* - sad (literally "under sorrow")
*   *faoi shéan* - happy, prosperous (literally "under a good omen")

### Temporal Meaning: 'around' or 'at'

`faoi` can refer to a general time period.
*   *faoi Nollaig* - at Christmas
*   *faoi Bhealtaine* - in May
*   *faoi seo* - by now
*   *faoin am seo* - at this time

### With Numbers: 'times'

`faoi` is used for multiplication.
*   *faoi dhó* - twice
*   *faoi sé* - six times
*   *A trí faoina ceathair sin a dó dhéag.* - Three times four is twelve.

### Other Meanings

*   **Against**: *faoin mballa* - against the wall
*   **Supporting**: *na colúin atá faoi* - the pillars that support it

## Dialectal Variants

The standard form is `faoi`. Other forms exist, particularly in Munster and Ulster.
*   **`faoi`**: Standard and Connacht. Also used in Ulster for the meaning "under".
*   **`fé`**: Common in Munster.
*   **`fá`**: An older standard form, still used in Ulster, often with the meaning "about" or "around".
*   **`fó`**, **`fí`**: Less common variants.

## Advanced Forms

### With Relative Clauses

`faoi` can combine with the relative particle `a` or the indirect relative form of the copula, `ar`.

| Tense | With Relative Pronoun `a` | With Copula `ar` | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Present | `faoina` | `faoinar` | under which |
| Past | `faoinar` | `faoinar` | under which |

Before a vowel, `faoinar` becomes `faoinarb` in the present and `faoinarbh` in the past.

*   *an cathaoir faoina bhfuil mo leabhar* - the chair under which my book is
*   *an crann faoina raibh fás* - the tree that grew (literally "the tree under which there was growth")

This structure is less common in modern Irish than ending the sentence with the preposition: *an chathaoir a bhfuil mo leabhar faoi*.

The variant `fá` has older relative forms without the linking `-n-`:
*   *A Róisín, ná bíodh brón ort fár éirigh duit.* - My Róisín, do not be sad about what happened to you.

### Asking Questions with 'faoi'

To form questions, `faoi` is used with interrogative words followed by an indirect relative clause.
*   `cé faoi...?` - under whom...?
*   `cad faoi / cé faoi / cá faoi...?` - under what...?
