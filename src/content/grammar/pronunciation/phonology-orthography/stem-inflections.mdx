In Irish, words change not only by adding endings (**external inflection**) but also by altering the word stem itself (**internal inflection**). These internal changes, or **stem inflections** (`infhillteacha`), are fundamental to Irish grammar.

Unlike the vowel changes (ablaut and umlaut) seen in languages like German, the most important stem changes in Irish involve the quality of the final consonant of the stem—specifically, whether it is "broad" or "slender". This change in consonant quality often causes related changes to the vowels in the stem to maintain spelling rules.

The main types of internal inflection are palatalisation (`caolú`), depalatalisation (`leathnú`), syncope (`coimriú`), and devoicing (`díghlórú`).

## Palatalisation (An Caolú)

Palatalisation, or `caolú` ("slenderising"), is the process where a broad consonant becomes a slender consonant. In writing, this is usually shown by adding an `i` next to the consonant to mark it as slender, according to the "slender with slender, broad with broad" spelling rule.

*   *bád* [bɑːd] (a boat) becomes *báid* [bɑːd′] (of a boat; boats)

Here, the broad `d` in `bád` becomes a slender `d` in `báid`.

This process can also cause more significant changes to the vowels within the stem. The primary change is always to the consonant's quality, but the spelling of the vowel must adapt.

| Broad Stem | Palatalised Stem | Example (Nom. → Gen./Pl.) |
| :--------- | :--------------- | :------------------------ |
| `-ea-`     | `-i-`            | *fear* → *fir* (man)      |
| `-ea-`     | `-ei-`           | *each* → *eich* (horse)   |
| `-éa-`     | `-éi-`           | *éan* → *éin* (bird)      |
| `-ia-`     | `-éi-`           | *iasc* → *éisc* (fish)    |
| `-ia-`     | `-iai-`          | *Brian* → *Briain* (Brian) |
| `-ío-`     | `-í-`            | *bóg* → *bíge* (chirping) |
| `-io-`     | `-i-`            | *fionn* → *finn* (bright) |
| `-iu-`     | `-i-`            | *fliuch* → *fliche* (wet) |

**Further Notes on Palatalisation:**

*   **Exceptions:** Some vowel changes are irregular, such as `mac` → `mic` (son) or `mall` → `moille` (slow).
*   **Dialectal Pronunciation:** The addition of an `i` can change the pronunciation of the preceding vowel. For example, in Connemara, `cnoc` [kruk] becomes `cnoic` [krik′] in the genitive.
*   **The `-ach` ending:** In polysyllabic words, the ending `-ach` or `-each` becomes `-aigh` or `-igh` through palatalisation.
    *   *báisteach* → *báistigh* (rain)
    *   *bacach* → *bacaigh* (lame person)
*   **Monosyllabic `-ch`:** In single-syllable words, `-ch` is retained.
    *   *déach* → *déich* (ten)
*   **Old Orthography:** In older spelling, a change from `-o-` to `-ui-` was common, e.g., `cnoc` → `cnuic` (now `cnoic`).

**Grammatical Uses of Palatalisation:**

*   Forming the genitive and vocative singular of first declension nouns (e.g., *an fear* → *an fhir*).
*   Forming the plural of many first declension nouns (e.g., *an fear* → *na fir*).
*   Forming the genitive singular of second declension nouns, along with an `-e` ending (e.g., *an lámh* → *na láimhe*).
*   Forming the comparative of first declension adjectives (e.g., *fliuch* → *fliche*).
*   Forming diminutives with the `-ín` suffix (e.g., *fear* → *firín*).

## Depalatalisation (An Leathnú)

Depalatalisation, or `leathnú` ("broadening"), is the opposite of palatalisation. A slender consonant becomes a broad consonant. In writing, this is often shown by dropping an `i`.

*   *abhainn* (a river) becomes *abhann* (of a river)

Here, the slender `-nn` in `abhainn` becomes a broad `-nn` in `abhann`.

Like palatalisation, this can cause related vowel changes.

| Slender Stem | Depalatalised Stem | Example (Nom. → Gen.) |
| :----------- | :----------------- | :-------------------- |
| `-ei-`       | `-ea-`             | *greim* → *greama* (bite) |
| `-éi-`       | `-éa-`             | *tincéir* → *tincéara* (tinker) |
| `-i-`        | `-ea-`             | *mil* → *meala* (honey) |
| `-io-`       | `-ea-`             | *fios* → *feasa* (knowledge) |
| `-í-`        | `-ío-`             | *feadaíl* → *feadaíola* (whistling) |
| `-ui-`       | `-o-`              | *fuil* → *fola* (blood) |
| `-ui-`       | `-u-`              | *cuir* → *cur* (to put) |

Another common example is *Éirinn* (Ireland) becoming *Éireann* in the genitive.

**Grammatical Uses of Depalatalisation:**

*   Forming the genitive of third declension nouns (e.g., *mil* → *meala*).
*   Forming the genitive of many fifth declension nouns (e.g., *Éirinn* → *Éireann*).
*   Forming the genitive of second declension adjectives (e.g., *bródúil* → *bródúla*).
*   Forming some verbal nouns (e.g., *cuir* → *cur*).
*   Broadening the stem of some first conjugation verbs before adding endings (e.g., *sábháil* → *sábhálann*).

## Syncope (An Coimriú)

Syncope, or `coimriú` ("shortening"), occurs in words with two or more syllables. It involves dropping the vowel of the final syllable of the stem when an ending is added. This shortens the stem from two syllables to one.

*   *imir* (to play) + `-íonn` → *imríonn* (plays)

Syncope happens when all three of the following conditions are met:

1.  The vowel to be dropped is in the last syllable of the stem and is **short, unstressed, and unaccented** (like the second `i` in `imir`).
2.  This vowel is positioned between a liquid consonant (`l`, `m`, `n`, `r`) and another consonant.
3.  An inflectional **ending** is added to the word (e.g., a verb ending or a plural noun ending).

**Further Rules and Effects of Syncope:**

*   **Vowel Changes:** Syncope can trigger changes in the first syllable to maintain spelling rules.
    *   *ramhar* → *raimhre* (fat)
    *   *domhain* → *doimhne* (deep)
*   **Consonant Shortening:** The double consonants `nn`, `ll`, and `rr` are shortened to `n`, `l`, and `r`.
    *   *aoibhinn* → *aoibhne* (delightful)
*   **Consonant Assimilation:** Certain consonant combinations are simplified.
    *   `ln`, `nl`, or `dl` become `ll`. Example: *álainn* → *áille* (beautiful).
    *   `dn` or `nd` become `nn`.
    *   An exception is `codail` → `codladh` (sleep), where `dl` is written but pronounced as `ll` ([koLə]).
*   **Associated Palatalisation:** Syncope is often accompanied by the palatalisation of adjacent consonants.
    *   *bóthar* → *bóithre* (road)
    *   *athair* → *aithreacha* (father)
*   **Devoicing:** A voiced `d` can become a voiceless `t` if the preceding consonant is `s`.
    *   *sluasaid* → *sluaiste* (shovel)

**Grammatical Uses of Syncope:**

*   In syncopating verbs (mostly second conjugation) before endings are added (e.g., *imir* → *imríonn*, *inis* → *insíonn*).
*   In adjective declension when adding endings like `-e` or `-a` (e.g., *an bhean ramhar* → *na mná raimhre*).

**Dialect Note: Syncope vs. Epenthetic Vowel**

In the Munster dialect, the effect of syncope is often neutralised in speech. An epenthetic (inserted) vowel is pronounced where the syncopated vowel was removed.

*   *imrím* (I play) is pronounced [im′**ə**ˈr′iːm′] in Munster, but [ˈim′r′iːm′] in other dialects.

## Devoicing (An Díghlórú)

Devoicing, or `díghlórú`, is the process where a voiced consonant (like `b`, `d`, `g`) changes to its voiceless counterpart (`p`, `t`, `k`).

Unlike some languages, Irish does not have general final devoicing (where a consonant at the end of a word becomes voiceless). For example, `scuab` ends with a clear [b] sound.

The main type of devoicing in Irish is **stem-final devoicing**. This happens when a voiced consonant at the end of a verb stem is followed by an ending that begins with an `h` sound, such as:

*   Future/conditional endings with `f` (pronounced [h]): `-faidh`, `-fadh`.
*   Verbal adjective endings with `th`: `-tha`.

When this occurs, the voiced consonant becomes voiceless, and the following `h` sound becomes silent. In modern spelling, this is sometimes reflected directly, e.g., `d` + `th` is written `dt`.

| :----------------- | :------------- | :------------ | :------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `b`                | `-bf-`         | `-bth-`       | [p]           | *scuabfaidh sé* (he will sweep), *scuabtha* (swept) |
| `d`                | `-df-`         | `-dt-`        | [t]           | *goidfidh sé* (he will steal), *goidte* (stolen) |
| `g`                | `-gf-`         | `-gth-`       | [k]           | *pógfaidh sé* (he will kiss), *pógtha* (kissed) |
| `bh`               | `-bhf-`        | `-f-`         | [f]           | *scríobhfaidh sé* (he will write), *scríofa* (written) |
| `mh`               | `-mhf-`        | `-f-`         | [f]           | *snámhfaidh sé* (he will swim), *snáfa* (swum) |

The consonants `l`, `n`, and `r` also become voiceless before an `h` sound.

Note that the sequence `-bhf-` resulting from devoicing is different from the eclipsis of `f-` (`bhfuil`), which is pronounced [v] or [w].
