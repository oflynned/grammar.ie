Irish has a different basic sentence structure to English. Understanding this "Verb-Subject-Object" order is the first step to forming correct sentences. This page outlines the fundamental rules for arranging words in an Irish sentence.

## Verb-Subject-Object (VSO)

The standard word order in an Irish sentence is **Verb - Subject - Object (VSO)**. This is a key difference from English, which uses a Subject-Verb-Object (SVO) structure.

*   *Buaileann an fear an madra.* - The man hits the dog.
    (Literally: Hits the man the dog.)
*   *Cheannaigh mé leabhar.* - I bought a book.
    (Literally: Bought I a book.)
*   *Tá an spéir gorm.* - The sky is blue.
    (Literally: Is the sky blue.)

## Adjectives

In Irish, adjectives almost always come **after** the noun they describe.

*   `an madra mór` - the big dog
*   `an spéir ghorm` - the blue sky
*   `an fear beag` - the little man

## Genitive Nouns

A noun in the genitive case, which often shows possession, also comes **after** the noun it belongs to.

*   `hata an fhir` - the man's hat
*   `madra an chailín` - the girl's dog
*   `barr an chnoic` - the top of the hill

## Possessive Pronouns

Unlike adjectives and genitive nouns, possessive pronouns (like "my", "your", "his") come **before** the noun.

*   `mo theach` - my house
*   `a mhadra` - his dog
*   `ár gcarr` - our car

## Adverbs

Adverbs, especially those indicating time and place, usually go at the **end** of the sentence.

*   *Cheannaigh mé leabhar inné.* - I bought a book yesterday.
*   *Tá mé anseo.* - I am here.
*   *Chonaic mé é ar an tsráid.* - I saw him on the street.

## Questions

Questions are typically formed by placing an interrogative particle or an interrogative pronoun at the very **beginning** of the sentence, before the verb.

*   *An bhfaca tú é?* - Did you see him?
*   *Cé a rinne é?* - Who did it?
*   *Cá bhfuil tú?* - Where are you?

## Negation

To make a sentence negative, you add a negative particle to the **beginning** of the sentence, before the verb.

*   *Ní fhaca mé é.* - I did not see him.
*   *Níor cheannaigh mé leabhar.* - I did not buy a book.
*   *Níl mé anseo.* - I am not here.
