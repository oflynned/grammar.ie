The verb `téigh` (to go) is one of the eleven irregular verbs in Irish. It is highly irregular, using several different stems across its various tenses and moods.

### The Stems of `téigh`

-   **`téigh-`**: Used for the present tense, imperfect tense, and imperative mood.
-   **`chuaigh-`**: The independent stem for the past tense (preterite). Note that this stem does not use a preterite particle like `d'`.
-   **`deach-`**: The dependent stem for the past tense (preterite), used after particles like `ní`, `an`, `go`, and `nach`.
-   **`rach-`**: Used for the future tense and the conditional mood.

#### Past Tense (Preterite)

The past tense uses the independent stem `chuaigh-` in positive statements and the dependent stem `deach-` in negative and interrogative forms.

**Independent Forms (Stem: `chuaigh-`)**

| Person | Form |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1st sg. | `chuas` |
| 2nd sg. | `chuais` |
| 3rd sg. | `chuaigh sé / sí` |
| 1st pl. | `chuamar` |
| 2nd pl. | `chuabhair` |
| 3rd pl. | `chuadar` |
| Autonomous | `chuathas` |

**Dependent Forms (Stem: `deach-`)**

| Particle | 1st sg. | 1st pl. | 3rd pl. | Autonomous |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `ní` | `ní dheachaigh mé` | `ní dheachamar` | `ní dheachadar` | `ní dheachthas` |
| `an` | `an ndeachaigh mé` | `an ndeachamar` | `an ndeachadar` | `an ndeachthas` |

For the 2nd and 3rd singular, and 2nd plural, the analytic form `(part.) deachaigh` + pronoun is used (e.g., `ní dheachaigh tú`, `an ndeachaigh sé`).

### Alternative and Dialectal Forms

-   **Imperative:** The 2nd person singular imperative `téigh` is often replaced by `téir`, `téire`, or `téirigh`.
-   **Present Tense:** The 3rd person singular form `téid` is sometimes used instead of `téann`.
-   **Connemara Irish:**
    -   Future and conditional forms often use the stem `gabh-`.
    -   The imperative forms are `gabh!` (singular) and `gabhigí!` (plural). Note that `gabh` usually means "to take".
    -   The verbal noun is `goil` or `gabháil`.
    -   The verbal adjective is `goite`.
-   **Munster Irish:** The stem `ragh-` is often used instead of `rach-` in the future and conditional.

### Orthography Note

In older, pre-reform spelling, some forms were written differently:
-   `chuaigh` was written as `chuaidh`.
-   `deachaigh` was written as `deachaidh`.

The verb `téigh` (to go) was distinguished from the regular verb `téigh` (to warm), which was written as `téidh` in the old orthography.
