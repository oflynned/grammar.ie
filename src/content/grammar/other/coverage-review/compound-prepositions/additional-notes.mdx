## Formation and Usage

Compound prepositions in Irish are phrases, typically consisting of two or more words, that function as a single preposition. They are usually formed from a simple preposition followed by a noun.

Because the final element of a compound preposition is a noun, any noun that follows the phrase must be in the genitive case.
