The verb `cluin` means "to hear". It is mostly regular, following the patterns of second-conjugation verbs.

Its only irregularity is in the past tense (preterite), where it uses the stem `chuala`. Note that the autonomous form, `chualathas`, is also lenited. The verb has no special dependent forms.

`cluin` is the variant of `clois` ("to hear") that is commonly used in Ulster Irish. Both verbs share the same irregular past tense form, `chuala`.

## Indicative Mood

### Present Tense
- **1st sg:** `cluinim`
- **2nd sg:** `cluineann tú`
- **3rd sg:** `cluineann sé`
- **1st pl:** `cluinimid`
- **2nd pl:** `cluineann sibh`
- **3rd pl:** `cluineann siad`
- **Autonomous:** `cluintear`

### Past Tense (Preterite)
This tense uses the irregular stem `chuala`.

- **1st sg:** `chuala mé`
- **2nd sg:** `chuala tú`
- **3rd sg:** `chuala sé`
- **1st pl:** `chualamar`
- **2nd pl:** `chuala sibh`
- **3rd pl:** `chuala siad`
- **Autonomous:** `chualathas`

### Imperfect Tense
- **1st sg:** `chluininn`
- **2nd sg:** `chluinteá`
- **3rd sg:** `chluineadh sé`
- **1st pl:** `chluinimis`
- **2nd pl:** `chluineadh sibh`
- **3rd pl:** `chluinidís`
- **Autonomous:** `chluintí`

### Future Tense
- **1st sg:** `cluinfidh mé`
- **2nd sg:** `cluinfidh tú`
- **3rd sg:** `cluinfidh sé`
- **1st pl:** `cluinfimid`
- **2nd pl:** `cluinfidh sibh`
- **3rd pl:** `cluinfidh siad`
- **Autonomous:** `cluinfear`

## Conditional Mood
