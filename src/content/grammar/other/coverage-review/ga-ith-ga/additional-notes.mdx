The verb `ith` (to eat) is one of the irregular verbs in Irish. Its irregularity is primarily in the future and conditional tenses, which use the stem `íos-`. It does not have special dependent forms.

## Other Verbal Forms

- **Verbal Noun:** `ithe`
- **Genitive of the Verbal Noun:**
  - As a substantive: `ithe`
  - As an infinitive: `ite`
- **Verbal Adjective:** `ite`

## Archaic Forms

An older, now rare, past tense form is `duaidh` (from Old Irish `do-fúaid`).

- *Duaidh sé.* (He ate.)
- `duamar` or `duadhamar` (We ate.)
