In Irish, one of the most common ways to express the perfect aspect—an action that has just been completed—is by using the prepositional phrase `tar éis` (after) with the verbal noun. This construction is often called the "after perfect".

It corresponds to the English "to have just done something".

## Formation

The structure combines a form of the verb `bí` (to be) with `tar éis` and the verbal noun of the main action. The tense of `bí` determines the tense of the entire phrase.

The general formula is:
**`[form of bí]` + `[subject]` + `tar éis` + `[verbal noun phrase]`**

### With Intransitive Verbs

For verbs that do not take a direct object, the structure is straightforward:

- *Tá an bus tar éis imeacht.* (The bus has just left.)
- *Bhí mé tar éis titim.* (I had just fallen.)

### With Transitive Verbs

For verbs that take a direct object, the most common structure places the object between `tar éis` and the verbal noun. The particle `a` is used before the verbal noun, causing lenition.

The formula is:
**`... tar éis` + `[object]` + `a` + `[lenited verbal noun]`**

- *Tá mé tar éis an leabhar a léamh.* (I have just read the book.)
- *Tá sí tar éis an doras a dhúnadh.* (She has just closed the door.)

## Usage with Different Tenses

The overall tense of the statement is controlled by the form of `bí`.

### Present Tense (`tá`)

This form is used to say that something has just happened.

- *Tá siad tar éis an dinnéar a ithe.* (They have just eaten the dinner.)
- *Tá an scannán tar éis tosú.* (The film has just started.)

### Past Tense (`bhí`)

This form is used for the past perfect, indicating that something had just happened before another point in the past.

- *Bhí sé tar éis an teach a fhágáil nuair a tháinig mé.* (He had just left the house when I arrived.)
- *Bhí an clog tar éis bualadh.* (The clock had just struck.)

### Future Tense (`beidh`)

This form is used for the future perfect, indicating that something will have just happened at a future point in time.

- *Beidh mé tar éis an obair a chríochnú faoin am sin.* (I will have just finished the work by that time.)
- *Beidh siad tar éis imeacht nuair a shroichfidh tú.* (They will have just left when you arrive.)
