The verb `bí` (to be) has several non-standard conjugations that are still used in some dialects, most notably in Munster Irish. These forms often differ from the *Caighdeán Oifigiúil* (Official Standard) and may include older features like the particle `do` with past tense verbs. This page provides a reference for these dialectal forms.

## Munster Conjugations

The following tables show common non-standard conjugations for `bí` as used in Munster.

### Indicative Tenses

| Person | Present | Past | Imperfect | Future |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | `táim` | `do bhíos` | `do bhínn` | `bead` |
| 2nd sg. | `tá(nn) tú`, `tás tú`, `táir`, `taoi` | `do bhís` | `do bhíteá` | `beir` |
| 3rd sg. | `tá sé` | `do bhí sé` | `do bhíodh sé` | `beidh sé` |
| 1st pl. | `táimid` | `do bhíomair` | `do bhímis` | `beimid` |
| 2nd pl. | `tá(nn) sibh`, `tathaoi` | `do bhíobhair` | `do bhíodh sibh` | `beidh sibh`, `beithí` |
| 3rd pl. | `táid` | `do bhíodar` | `do bhídís` | `beid` |
| Autonomous | `táthar`, `táthars` | `do bhíothas` | `do bhítí` | `beifear` |

### Conditional and Imperative

| Person | Conditional | Imperative |
|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | `do bheinn` | `bím` |
| 2nd sg. | `do bheifeá` | `bí` |
| 3rd sg. | `do bheadh sé` | `bíodh sé` |
| 1st pl. | `do bheimis` | `bímis` |
| 2nd pl. | `do bheadh sibh` | `bígí`, `bídh` |
| 3rd pl. | `do bheidís` | `bídís` |
| Autonomous | `do bheifí` | `bítear` |

### Subjunctive Mood

| Person | Present Subjunctive | Habitual Present Subjunctive | Past Subjunctive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | `go rabhad` | `go mbíod` | `dá mbínn` |
| 2nd sg. | `go raibhir` | `go mbír` | `dá mbíteá` |
| 3rd sg. | `go raibh sé` | `go mbí sé` | `dá mbíodh sé` |
| 1st pl. | `go rabhaimid` | `go mbímid` | `dá mbímis` |
| 2nd pl. | `go raibh sibh`, `rabhtaoi` | `go mbí sibh` | `dá mbíodh sibh` |
| 3rd pl. | `go raibhid` | `go mbíd` | `dá mbídís` |
| Autonomous | `go rabhthar` | `go mbítear` | `dá mbítí` |

## Habitual Present and Dependent Forms

Dialectal forms also exist for the habitual present and for dependent forms used after particles.

### Habitual Present

| Person | Form |
|---|---|
| 1st sg. | `bím` |
| 2nd sg. | `bíonn tú`, `bír` |
| 3rd sg. | `bíonn sé` |
| 1st pl. | `bímid` |
| 2nd pl. | `bíonn sibh`, `bíthí` |
| 3rd pl. | `bíd` |
| Autonomous | `bítear` |

### Dependent Present Tense

These `fuil` forms are used after particles like `an` (question), `go` (that), `nach` (that...not), `ní` (not), and `ná` (negative question).

| Person | Positive (`an`/`go`/`nach`) | Negative (`ní`) | Negative Interrogative (`ná`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | `an bhfuilim` | `nílim` | `ná fuilim` |
| 2nd sg. | `an bhfuil(eann) tú`, `an bhfuilir` | `níl(eann) tú`, `nílir` | `ná fuil(eann) tú`, `ná fuilir` |
| 3rd sg. | `an bhfuil sé` | `níl sé` | `ná fuil sé` |
| 1st pl. | `an bhfuilimid` | `nílimid` | `ná fuilimid` |
| 2nd pl. | `an bhfuil(eann) sibh`, `an bhfuiltí` | `níl(eann) sibh`, `níltí` | `ná fuil(eann) sibh`, `ná fuiltí` |
| 3rd pl. | `an bhfuilid` | `nílid` | `ná fuilid` |
| Autonomous | `an bhfuiltear` | `níltear` | `ná fuiltear` |

### Dependent Past Tense

These `raibh` forms are used after particles in the past tense.

| Person | Form |
|---|---|
| 1st sg. | `an rabhas` |
| 2nd sg. | `an rabhais` |
| 3rd sg. | `an raibh sé` |
| 1st pl. | `an rabhamair` |
| 2nd pl. | `an rabhabhair` |
| 3rd pl. | `an rabhadar` |
| Autonomous | `an rabhthas` |

## Pronunciation and Usage Notes

<Dialect region="Munster">
In Ring (Co. Waterford) and sometimes in West Munster, the forms of `tá` are pronounced as if they were `thá` [hɑː].

The `-nn` ending in the present tense forms like `tánn`, `níleann`, and `bhfuileann` is typically only found in the 2nd person singular and plural (e.g., `tánn tú`, `tánn sibh`).
</Dialect>

The use of `ná` as a negative interrogative particle is another feature of Munster Irish.

<Dialect region="Munster">
In most of Munster (except for Waterford), the particle `ná` is used instead of `nach` for negative questions (e.g., "are you not...?"). This `ná` does not cause any initial mutation on the following verb.
</Dialect>
