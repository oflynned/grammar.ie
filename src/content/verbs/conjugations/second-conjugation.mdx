## Overview

The second conjugation, or `An Dara Réimniú`, primarily includes polysyllabic verbs. These verbs follow a distinct pattern for their endings, particularly in the future and conditional tenses, which are marked by `-eoidh`/`-óidh` and `-eodh`/`-ódh` respectively.

### Verb Categories

#### Verbs in -igh

This is the largest group and includes all polysyllabic verbs ending in `-igh`.

- *coinnigh* (to hold)
- *salaigh* (to soil)
- *fiafraigh* (to ask)

#### Syncopated Verbs

These are polysyllabic verbs with stems ending in `-il`, `-in`, `-ir`, or `-is` (when following l, n, r). The second syllable of the stem is dropped (syncopated) before adding endings.

- *imir* becomes *imr-*
- *oscail* becomes *oscl-*
- *inis* becomes *ins-*

Some verbs in this category do not syncopate but instead undergo partial depalatalisation.

- *freastail* (to attend) -> *freastalaíonn sé*
- *taistil* (to travel) -> *taistealaíonn sé*

Historically, the verb `éirigh` (to rise) was a syncopated verb (`éirgh-`), but today it is treated as a regular `-igh` verb: `éiríonn sé`.

#### Other Polysyllabic Verbs

A smaller group of verbs, often ending in `-im` or `-ing`, also belong to this conjugation. In the standard language, they do not syncopate.

- *foghlaim* (to learn)
- *tarraing* (to pull)
- *fulaing* (to endure)

#### Verbs with Epenthetic Vowels

The verb `lorg` (to seek) is treated as a disyllabic verb [lorəg] and thus belongs to the second conjugation. However, other verbs with an epenthetic vowel, such as `dearg` (to redden) or `searg` (to wither), belong to the first conjugation in the standard language.

### Exclusions

The following verb types are **not** part of the second conjugation:

- **Monosyllabic verbs:** These always belong to the first conjugation, even if they end in `-igh`.
- **Polysyllabic verbs in -áil:** These also belong to the first conjugation.

## Conjugation Patterns

### Slender Final Verbs (-igh)

These verbs have a slender vowel (i or e) before the final `-igh`.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1.Sg. | `-ím` | `-íos` | `-ínn` | `-eod` | `-eoinn` | `-ím` | `-í` mé | `-ínn` |
| 2.Sg. | `-íonn` tú | `-ís` | `-íteá` | `-eoidh` tú | `-eofá` | `-igh` | `-í` tú | `-íteá` |
| 3.Sg. | `-íonn` sé | `-igh` sé | `-íodh` sé | `-eoidh` sé | `-eodh` sé | `-íodh` sé | `-í` sé | `-íodh` sé |
| 1.Pl. | `-ímid` | `-íomar` | `-ímis` | `-eoimid` | `-eoimis` | `-ímis` | `-ímid` | `-ímis` |
| 2.Pl. | `-íonn` sibh | `-íobhair` | `-íodh` sibh | `-eoidh` sibh | `-eodh` sibh | `-ígí` | `-í` sibh | `-íodh` sibh |
| 3.Pl. | `-íonn` siad | `-íodar` | `-ídís` | `-eoidh` siad | `-eoidís` | `-ídís` | `-í` siad | `-ídís` |
| Auto. | `-ítear` | `-íodh` | `-ítí` | `-eofar` | `-eofaí` | `-ítear` | `-ítear` | `-ítí` |
| Rel. | `-íos` | - | - | `-eos` | - | - | - | - |

## Dialectal Variations

- **Munster:** Some verbs may end in `-imh` instead of `-igh`, such as *coinnimh* for the standard *coinnigh*.
- **Ulster:** Some verbs may end in `-óigh`, such as *éalóigh* and *fostóigh* for the standard *éalaigh* and *fostaigh*.
- In some dialects, an `-n-` is inserted before the ending of certain verbs, for example: *foghlaimníonn sé* instead of *foghlaimíonn sé*.
