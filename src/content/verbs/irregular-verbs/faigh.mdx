## Overview

The verb `faigh` is an irregular verb with different stems for different tenses. The preterite uses the stem `fuair`, while the future and conditional use the stem `geobh-`. It also has special dependent forms in the future, conditional, and preterite tenses.

## Historical Forms and Mutations

### Historical Context

Older forms of the verb distinguished between absolute and dependent forms in the present and imperfect tenses. The absolute forms, now largely obsolete, were `(do-)gheibheann` and `(do-)gheibheadh`.

|               | Dependent   | Absolute          |
|:--------------|:------------|:------------------|
| **Present**   | `faigheann` | `(do-)gheibheann` |
| **Imperfect** | `faigheadh` | `(do-)gheibheadh` |

The lenition seen in the modern future and conditional stems (`gheobh-`) originates from the historical prefix `do-`, which was once common in these forms (e.g., `(do-)gheobhaidh sé`).

### Mutation Rules

The preterite form `fuair` is never lenited. For example, with the direct relative particle `a`, it remains `a fuair`.

- *An leabhar a fuair mé inné.* (The book that I got yesterday.)

However, `fuair` is eclipsed after particles like `ní`, `an`, and `go`. The spelling `ní bhfuair` is an adaptation of a phonetic process. Historically, this was a lenition (`ní fhuair`), and the `bh` sound arose as a glide between `ní` and `fhuair`. The modern spelling reflects this sound with an eclipsis.

## Dialectal Variations

### Ulster

- **Present:** The 3rd person absolute form is usually the endingless `gheibh sé`. The dependent form is `ní fhánn sé`. The form `fánn` is also sometimes used as an absolute form. The autonomous is `gheibhthear` or `faghthar`.
- **Future:** The absolute is `gheobhaidh sé`, while the dependent is `ní bhfuighidh sé`.
- **Imperfect:** The absolute is `gheibheadh`, and the dependent is `ní bhfuigheadh`.
- **Preterite:** The form is `fuair`. The negative can also be `chan fhuair`.
- **Imperative:** The 2nd person is `fagh` or `faigh`, and the 3rd person is `faghadh sé`.
- **Present Subjunctive:** `go bhfaghaidh sé`.

### Connacht

- **Present:** The form `fánn` is common for both absolute and dependent. Other forms include `gheibheann`, `geibhfeann`, and `geobhfann`. The eclipsed dependent form `bhfaigheann` or `bhfuigheann` also occurs.
- **Preterite:** An alternative autonomous form, `fríth`, is sometimes used instead of `fuarthas`.
- **Imperative:** The 3rd person is `faigheadh` or `faigheadh sé`. Another form is `gheobhfeadh`.
