## Overview

The verb `ith` means "to eat". It is considered an irregular verb because its stem changes to `íos-` in the future and conditional tenses. It does not have any special dependent forms.

## Historical Forms

An older, now rare, preterite form of `ith` is `duaidh`, which comes from the Old Irish `do-fúaid`.

- *Duaidh sé* (he ate)
- *Duamar* or *duadhamar* (we ate)
